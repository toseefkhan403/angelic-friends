## 5. Angel Subscriptions and Credits

- Becoming an "Angel" is a recurring monthly subscription purchased through Google
  Play or the App Store, at the tier you choose. It grants you a monthly allotment of
  credits.
- Unused credits roll over to the next month as long as your subscription stays
  active.
- Subscriptions renew automatically at the price and interval shown at purchase,
  unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period,
  through Google Play / the App Store or Profile → Manage subscription.
- If your subscription lapses (expires, is cancelled, or a payment fails), your
  pledges pause — they stop counting toward a dog's funding meter and chat access
  pauses — but your pledged amounts are preserved and resume automatically if you
  resubscribe.

## 6. Pledging Credits

When you pledge credits to a dog or to the feeding fund, that pledge is **permanent
and cannot be moved, refunded as credits, or reassigned to a different dog** — this
is because a dog's page publicly shows its funding progress, and we want that number
to be stable and trustworthy for everyone who visits it. Only pledge credits to a dog
or cause you're sure about.
